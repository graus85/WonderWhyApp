# Privacy policy — Wonder Why

_Last updated: September 20, 2026_

Wonder Why is designed to be simple and to respect your privacy.

## What we collect

Nothing. There is no account to create and no server behind the app, and no information about you is sent to us or to anyone else.

## Where your data stays

Your progress — days read, badges, saved whys, and your language, appearance and notification preferences — stays only on this iPhone. There is no account or sync, so if you delete the app, or change device without an iPhone backup, it is lost. You can also erase your progress at any time with “Reset progress” in Settings.

## Notifications

If you turn on the daily notification, the app asks iOS for permission and schedules the reminders on your iPhone, at the time you choose. They are created entirely on the device, with no server involved. You can turn them off at any time in the app's Settings or in iOS Settings.

## No tracking

Wonder Why has no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and it never builds a profile of you.

## Sharing

The Share button creates an image of the why you chose, together with its text and a link to the app, and sends them to the app you pick — only when you tap it. The image is drawn on your iPhone and is never uploaded to any server. Nothing is ever shared automatically.

## Contact

For any question about this policy you can write to: wonderwhyapp@gmail.com
